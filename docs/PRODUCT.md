# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers who want a terminal-themed portfolio website without building one from scratch. They want something that looks distinctive, not another template. They're technical enough to appreciate the terminal metaphor but want the output to feel polished, not gimmicky.

## Product Purpose

Terminal Portfolio turns a developer's GitHub presence into a living, themed portfolio. It syncs repos, contributions, and READMEs automatically, applies a terminal-inspired design system, and handles deployment. The landing page (this surface) exists to show what the platform can do — not by telling, but by embodying the same craft it offers.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, editorial, refined. Quiet confidence. The terminal is not a costume — it's a design constraint that produces clarity.

## Anti-references

- No SaaS cliches: no hero metrics, no pricing tables, no "10K+ users" badges, no gradient text
- No AI-generated copy — every word earns its place
- No glassmorphism or decorative blurs
- No side-stripe borders or card grids with icon + heading + text repeated endlessly
- No "Startup landing page" template DNA
- No monospace as lazy shorthand for "developer"

## Design Principles

- **Practice what you preach**: the landing page should demonstrate the same craft the platform enables. If it offers terminal beauty, the page itself must be beautiful.
- **Show, don't tell**: demonstrate features through real working UI, not feature lists. The terminal in the hero should be functional, not a screenshot.
- **Editorial restraint**: generous whitespace, deliberate typography, a single clear idea per section. No cramming.
- **Expert confidence**: no overselling, no exclamation points, no "revolutionary" or "game-changing." Describe what it does and let the work speak.
- **Terminal as method, not theme**: the aesthetic comes from terminal-native constraints (mono spacing, cursor fidelity, command-line clarity), not from adding green text on a dark background.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum
- Bilingual (English and Arabic) with full RTL support — already built into the platform, must be reflected on the landing page
- Respect reduced motion preferences
- Don't rely on color alone to convey information
